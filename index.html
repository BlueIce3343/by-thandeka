Our story

Baked with Thandeka's love

What started as a family passion became something the whole neighbourhood fell in love with. Every item that leaves our kitchen is made by hand, the old-fashioned way — no shortcuts, no preservatives, just honest baking.

Whether it's a birthday, a gathering, or just because you deserve a treat — By Thandeka is here to make it sweeter. Based right here in Pietermaritzburg, baked with pride.
